The composition in question is called "Wind of the Rainforest" and was created in 2019 by Kevin MacLeod of Green Bay, WI.

The author had waived his copyright to the composition and dedicated it to the Public Domain under the permanent, irrevocable, no-attribution Creative Commons Zero (CC0) license.

The composition was then published on FreePD.com — an archive of copyright-free, public-domain, no-attribution music.
